# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 21, 2026_

Swift TODO does not collect, send or sell any data. It has no servers, no analytics and no accounts.

## What is stored, and where

Your to-dos are stored with your browser's extension storage:

- **Browser sync storage** (`storage.sync`), which your browser syncs between your devices through your browser account (Google, Microsoft or Mozilla), if you have sync turned on. That sync is run by your browser and covered by its privacy policy, not by Swift TODO.
- **Local extension storage** (`storage.local`), which stays on your device. It holds to-dos you keep on this device, changes waiting to be synced, the trash, which reminders were already shown, the list you last had open, and whether focus mode is on and which to-dos it skipped.
- **Session storage** (`storage.session`), cleared when the browser closes. It holds a running focus timer.

Your settings (skin, reminders and so on) are synced like your to-dos. The popup also keeps a copy of your skin and theme, so it opens without a flash, and which sections you folded, in its own page storage on your device. When you add a to-do from a page, the page's address and title are saved with it, so you can go back to the page; they stay in your storage like the rest of the to-do. Exports are files saved on your device, and imports are read on your device; neither is sent anywhere.

Uninstalling the extension removes its storage from that browser. What happens to synced copies is up to your browser's sync.

## Permissions

- **Storage**: to save your to-dos, as described above.
- **Alarms**: to finish saving changes to sync shortly after the popup closes, and to wake up for reminders and the focus timer.
- **Context menus**: to add "Add to Swift TODO" to the right-click menu on selected text.
- **Active tab** and **scripting**: when you pick "Add to Swift TODO", to read the text you selected on that tab. Nothing runs on pages otherwise.
- **Notifications** (optional, only if you turn reminders on): to show reminders, the daily digest and the end of a focus timer.

## The website

The website, [swift-todo.ouss.es](https://swift-todo.ouss.es), is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics, and its fonts and images come from the site itself. The trailer is played from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode, and nothing loads from YouTube until you press play; from then on, YouTube's privacy policy applies. GitHub may keep server logs of visits, as described in its privacy statement.
