# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 21, 2026_

Swift TODO does not collect, send or sell any data. It has no servers, no analytics and no accounts.

## What is stored, and where

Your to-dos are stored with your browser's extension storage:

- **Browser sync storage** (`storage.sync`), which your browser syncs between your devices through your browser account (Google, Microsoft or Mozilla), if you have sync turned on. That sync is run by your browser and covered by its privacy policy, not by Swift TODO.
- **Local extension storage** (`storage.local`), which stays on your device. It holds to-dos you keep on this device, changes waiting to be synced, the trash, and which reminders were already shown.
- **Session storage** (`storage.session`), cleared when the browser closes. It holds a running focus timer.

Your settings (skin, reminders and so on) are synced like your to-dos. When you add a to-do from a page, the page's address and title are saved with it, so you can go back to the page; they stay in your storage like the rest of the to-do. Exports are files saved on your device, and imports are read on your device; neither is sent anywhere.

Uninstalling the extension removes its storage from that browser. What happens to synced copies is up to your browser's sync.

## Permissions

- **Storage**: to save your to-dos, as described above.
- **Alarms**: to finish saving changes to sync shortly after the popup closes, and to wake up for reminders and the focus timer.
- **Context menus**: to add "Add to Swift TODO" to the right-click menu on selected text.
- **Active tab** and **scripting**: when you pick "Add to Swift TODO", to read the text you selected on that tab. Nothing runs on pages otherwise.
- **Notifications** (optional, only if you turn reminders on): to show reminders, the daily digest and the end of a focus timer.
